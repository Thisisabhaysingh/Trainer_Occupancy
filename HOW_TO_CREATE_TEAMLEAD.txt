=====================================================
HOW TO CREATE AND MANAGE TEAM LEAD USERS
=====================================================

OVERVIEW:
---------
Team Leads are a new role between Managers and Employees. Each department can have one Team Lead who manages employees within that specific department.

=====================================================
METHOD 1: CREATE TEAM LEAD VIA ADMIN DASHBOARD (RECOMMENDED)
=====================================================

Step 1: Login as Admin
-----------------------
1. Go to your application URL
2. Login with admin credentials
3. You will be redirected to the Admin Dashboard

Step 2: Navigate to Manage Employees
-------------------------------------
1. Click on "Manage Employees" tab in the Admin Dashboard
2. You will see all existing employees listed

Step 3: Add New Team Lead
--------------------------
1. Click on the "+ Add Employee" card
2. Fill in the form:
   - Employee Code: e.g., "TL001" (must be unique)
   - Name: Full name of the team lead
   - Email: Valid email address (must be unique)
   - Department: Select the department they will lead
   - Role: Select "Team Lead" from the dropdown
   - Password: Set a secure password
3. Click "Add Employee" button
4. The team lead user will be created

Step 4: Verify Team Lead Creation
----------------------------------
1. The new team lead should appear in the employee list
2. Their role should show as "teamlead"
3. They can now login using their email and password

=====================================================
METHOD 2: EDIT EXISTING EMPLOYEE TO TEAM LEAD
=====================================================

Step 1: Login as Admin
-----------------------
1. Go to your application URL
2. Login with admin credentials

Step 2: Find the Employee
--------------------------
1. Go to "Manage Employees" section
2. Find the employee you want to promote to Team Lead
3. Click the "Edit" button on their card

Step 3: Change Role to Team Lead
---------------------------------
1. In the Edit Employee dialog:
   - Keep Name, Email, and Department as is
   - Change Role dropdown to "Team Lead"
2. Click "Update Employee"
3. The employee is now a Team Lead

Step 4: Verify the Change
--------------------------
1. The employee's role should now show as "teamlead"
2. They can login and access the Team Lead dashboard

=====================================================
METHOD 4: CREATE TEAM LEAD VIA DATABASE (DIRECT SQL)
=====================================================

IMPORTANT: Only use this method if you have direct database access and understand SQL.

Step 1: Hash the Password
--------------------------
Use bcrypt to hash the password. You can use an online bcrypt generator or Node.js:

const bcrypt = require('bcryptjs');
const hashedPassword = await bcrypt.hash('YourPassword123', 10);
console.log(hashedPassword);

Step 2: Insert Team Lead User
------------------------------
INSERT INTO `users` (
  `employeeCode`, 
  `name`, 
  `email`, 
  `password`, 
  `role`, 
  `departmentId`, 
  `branchId`, 
  `createdAt`, 
  `updatedAt`
)
VALUES (
  'TL001',
  'John Doe',
  'john.doe@company.com',
  '$2a$10$hashedPasswordHere',  -- Use the hashed password from Step 1
  'teamlead',
  1,  -- Department ID
  1,  -- Branch ID (optional)
  NOW(),
  NOW()
);

Step 3: Assign Team Lead to Department (Optional)
--------------------------------------------------
UPDATE `departments` 
SET `teamLeadId` = 'TL001' 
WHERE `id` = 1;

=====================================================
TEAM LEAD LOGIN AND ACCESS
=====================================================

How Team Leads Login:
----------------------
1. Go to the login page
2. Enter their email and password
3. They will be automatically redirected to /teamlead dashboard
4. They will see their department's analytics and employees

What Team Leads Can Do:
------------------------
✓ View department analytics
✓ View all employees in their department
✓ View all activities in their department
✓ Assign activities to employees
✓ Track employee performance
✓ View day-wise hours and productivity trends

What Team Leads CANNOT Do:
---------------------------
✗ Create new users (only admin and manager can)
✗ Access other departments' data
✗ Manage employees outside their department
✗ Delete users or departments
✗ Access admin or manager dashboards

=====================================================
ASSIGNING TEAM LEAD TO DEPARTMENT
=====================================================

Via Admin Dashboard:
--------------------
Currently, you need to assign team leads to departments via database.

Via Database:
-------------
UPDATE `departments` 
SET `teamLeadId` = 'TL001'  -- Employee code of the team lead
WHERE `id` = 1;  -- Department ID

Verify Assignment:
------------------
SELECT 
  d.id,
  d.name AS department_name,
  d.teamLeadId,
  u.name AS team_lead_name
FROM `departments` d
LEFT JOIN `users` u ON d.teamLeadId = u.employeeCode
WHERE d.id = 1;

=====================================================
MANAGING MULTIPLE TEAM LEADS
=====================================================

One Team Lead Per Department:
------------------------------
- Each department should have only ONE team lead
- If you assign a new team lead to a department, it will replace the previous one

Multiple Departments with Team Leads:
--------------------------------------
- You can have multiple team leads in your organization
- Each team lead manages their own department
- Team leads cannot see other departments' data

Example Structure:
------------------
Branch: Mumbai
  └── Department: Coding
      └── Team Lead: TL001 (John Doe)
      └── Employees: EMP001, EMP002, EMP003
  └── Department: Mechatronics
      └── Team Lead: TL002 (Jane Smith)
      └── Employees: EMP004, EMP005

Branch: Delhi
  └── Department: Electronics
      └── Team Lead: TL003 (Bob Johnson)
      └── Employees: EMP006, EMP007

=====================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
=====================================================

Issue: Team Lead Cannot Login
------------------------------
Solution:
1. Verify the user exists in the database
2. Check that role is set to 'teamlead' (not 'team lead' or 'TeamLead')
3. Verify email and password are correct
4. Check database migrations were applied

Issue: Team Lead Sees No Data
------------------------------
Solution:
1. Verify team lead has a departmentId assigned
2. Check that department has employees
3. Ensure activities exist for that department
4. Verify foreign key relationships are correct

Issue: Team Lead Can Access Admin Routes
-----------------------------------------
Solution:
1. Check that role is exactly 'teamlead' in database
2. Verify middleware is checking roles correctly
3. Clear browser cookies and login again

Issue: "Unknown column 'branchId'" Error
-----------------------------------------
Solution:
1. Run the database migration queries
2. Ensure branchId column exists in users table
3. Restart your server after migrations

=====================================================
BEST PRACTICES
=====================================================

1. Use Descriptive Employee Codes:
   - Good: TL_CODING_001, TL_MECH_001
   - Bad: TL1, TEAMLEAD1

2. Assign Team Leads to Departments:
   - Always link team leads to their departments
   - Update department.teamLeadId field

3. Set Strong Passwords:
   - Minimum 8 characters
   - Mix of letters, numbers, and symbols

4. Document Team Lead Assignments:
   - Keep a record of which team lead manages which department
   - Update documentation when changes occur

5. Regular Access Reviews:
   - Periodically review team lead permissions
   - Remove access when team leads change roles
